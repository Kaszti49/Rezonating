# 8.192 MHz oscillator

**Why we need it:** Required clock for the ADS131M04 (no internal oscillator). 3.3 V CMOS output.

## Notes
- **Chosen part:** TXC/QST QTM325-8.192MBD-T, 8.192 MHz, 3.3 V CMOS, plus-minus 25 ppm, -40 to 85 C, enable/disable pin, 4-SMD no-lead 3.2 x 2.5 mm. DigiKey AT on 2026-10-06: in stock, 1 week lead time, count unconfirmed (one read showed 12, another showed no count; we need 2).
- **Wiring (QTM325 datasheet Rev. 1.04):** pin 1 is enable; tie it high (output is on at logic 1). CMOS output, VOH at least 90% and VOL at most 10% of VDD, 3.3 V supply, which matches the ADC's DVDD.
- **Pinout:** pin 1 = enable (datasheet: output on at logic 1 or open, off at logic 0; VIH ≥ 70% VDD). Pins 2 GND, 3 OUT, 4 VDD follow the usual 4-pad oscillator layout; check them against the datasheet drawing before soldering.
- **Hand-soldering:** 3.2 x 2.5 mm leadless is hard by hand; it goes on the [[Oscillator adapter board]] (or use hot air). No DIP or large-SMD part at 8.192 MHz was found in stock in the EU.
- **Easier 7 x 5 mm options (all out of stock or unverified):**
  - TXC 7W-8.192MBE-T, 7.0 x 5.0 mm, 3.3 V, plus-minus 50 ppm: stock not found. TXC 7W-8.192MBA-T / QTM750-8.192MBA-T at Farnell: 0 in stock (findchips, 2026-10-05).
  - CTS CB3LV-3I-8M192000, 7 x 5 mm, 3.3 V: DigiKey shows 0 in stock, 11 weeks lead, next 1,000 units 2026-12-28. The price shown (1.29 at 1, 0.12 at 10) looks like a page error.
  - Renesas XLH736008-192000X, 7.5 x 5.2 mm: 0 stock, no backorders.
  - Crystek C3390-8.192: not stocked, 9 weeks.
- **Other small options seen:** TXC QTM216J / QTM252J-8.192MBJ-T (2.0 to 2.5 mm), 78 to 81 in stock at Farnell, USD 1.0 to 1.3. Even smaller.
- **Cheap fallback (inferred):** the ESP32-S3 could output 8.192 MHz as a clock itself, but jitter may hurt ADC noise; not tested. Not a recommendation.
- **Fallback modules:** no ready-made ADS131M04 module found (only the 257 EUR EVM, see the ADC note).
- TME and Mouser EU pages could not be read (403 or timeout), so no TME or Mouser stock was verified.
- 2026-10-06: already in the lab (Kaszti, after talking with the boss); not part of ORD-001. Kept for later use.
