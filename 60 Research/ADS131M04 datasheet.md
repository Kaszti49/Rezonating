# ADS131M04 datasheet

**One line:** TI 4-channel simultaneous 24-bit ADC.

## Key numbers
- Needs 8.192 MHz CLKIN (no internal oscillator)
- 500 SPS to 64 kSPS
- FSR ±1.2 V / gain, gain up to 128
- Inputs: AGND −1.6 V to AVDD +0.3 V

## What it means for us
Core of the node. Read before soldering and before writing the driver.
