# Charter

**Rezonating turns floors, walls and machines into sensors.** One vibration-sensing node with swappable heads; software packs decide what it detects. No cameras, no microphones.

## Year 1 goal
One indoor node that recognises **who is walking** from floor vibration, detects **falls** with the same hardware, and proves a **second head type** with the appliance pack.

## Success at the end of Year 1
| Pack | Target |
| --- | --- |
| [[footstep-id]] | ≥ 85% correct with 3 people on our own floor |
| [[fall]] | ≥ 95% of dummy falls caught, < 1 false alarm per week |
| [[appliance]] | Flags an induced imbalance in 9 of 10 runs |

## Not in Year 1
Field node, LoRa, battery and solar, hive, barn, rodents, tap surface, own app. They stay in the idea list until Year 1 results are in ([[D-010 Plan Year 1 only]]).

## Budget
€500 for Year 1 hardware, paid by the company. The dashboard tracks it from the part and order notes. Change `budget_eur` above if it changes.
