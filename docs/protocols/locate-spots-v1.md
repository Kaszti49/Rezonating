# Protocol: locate-spots-v1

Which side of the device did a step come from? Steps in place at eight marked spots. The sidecar's
`protocol` field must say `locate-spots-v1`.

Machine-readable version: `locate-spots-v1.yaml` (the Lab's protocol runner reads it).
Change both files together.

## Before

- Written consent from everyone recorded. Assign each a pseudonym (P1, P2, …);
  the name list stays off the repo.
- Mark the spots on the floor: left, right, front and back of the device, at 1 m and at 2 m.
  FRONT is marked on the lid.
- Write down the floor material, the shoes, where the case stood, and whether it was moved since the last session.
- The case stands flat and both geophones are firm in their cups.

## The round

30 s of silence, then for each person, at 1 m left, right, front, back and then at 2 m in the same order:

1. Walk to the spot in a wide arc, not past the case (10 s, labelled `silence`). Stand still.
2. Step in place, about ten ordinary steps, no jumps (10 s, labelled with the spot).

## Rules

- **Two rounds.** Limits or models are chosen on the first; the second, on another day or after a
  pause, is the only one that gives an accuracy figure.
- The `silence` between spots contains the walk to the spot: it is not quiet floor. Use the first 30 s for that.
- A step counts as left or right only; front, back and too far are one answer for two geophones on a line.
